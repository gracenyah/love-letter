Hi, my love,

I’m writing this because I’m a lot better at feeling things than at saying them, and you deserve to hear it properly. So, get comfy. Maybe grab a snack. This one’s long, and it’s all about you.

Why you matter to me

I don’t think you realise how much room you take up in my heart. You move through my days so quietly, and somehow everything feels softer because you’re in them. I love who you are: the way your mind works, the odd little things only you find funny, the way you try, even when you think nobody’s watching. I’m watching. I always notice.

The things I don’t always say

Sometimes I see something tiny, a song, a silly video, a stranger laughing the way you do, and my first thought is I have to tell him. I love it when I have your attention. I love being the person you tell things to. I just don’t say it nearly enough, so here it is in writing, where you can’t tease me about it. (You can tease me a little.)

I love being your girl

I really do. I love the little flutter when your name lights up my phone. I love how safe your hugs feel, and how we can go quiet next to each other and it’s still my favourite conversation. Having someone to call mine, someone I get to say “my boyfriend” about with a ridiculously proud smile, still makes me a bit giddy. I’m happy. Properly, softly, all-the-way happy.

Even on the difficult days

We’re not perfect, and I don’t want us to pretend we are. There will be days we misunderstand each other, days one of us is tired and says it the wrong way. On those days I want us to talk instead of going quiet. I want to be patient with you, and I’ll need you to be patient with me too. I’d rather work it out together than be right on my own.

My favourite things about you

  • The way you listen as if what I’m saying matters, because to you, it does.
  • Your laugh, especially the one you try to hold back.
  • How you turn ordinary evenings into plans I look forward to.
  • The second you pull me close and the whole world gets quieter.
  • How hard you try, even when you’d never say so.
  • The things I admire and forget to mention: your kindness, your steadiness, your effort.

If I could make you understand one thing

It would be this: you are wanted. You are valued. You are loved. I don’t take having you for granted, not for a single day, and I hope you never have to wonder whether you matter to me. You do. So much.

Your girl ❤️

For your difficult days

Hey, you.

If you’re reading this, it’s probably a heavy day. First: I’m proud of you for getting through it, and I’m sorry it’s hard.

You don’t have to be perfect to be loved. You don’t have to be strong every hour, or have it all figured out. I didn’t fall for the version of you with no bad days. I fell for you, and that includes the tired, quiet, frustrated and unsure parts.

You deserve tenderness. You deserve patience, rest, and somebody on your side. I’m on your side. Take a breath, drink some water, and let yourself be held, even if it’s just by these words for now. Then call me. I’d love to hear your voice.

Always yours ❤️

If life gives me the opportunity to choose again, I hope I get to find you, fall for you and be your girl all over again.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being mine. And thank you for giving me another beautiful reason to smile.

All my love,
Your girl. ❤️
